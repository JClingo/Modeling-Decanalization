\subsection{Implementation settings}\label{app:implementation-settings}

Table~\ref{tab:world-settings} lists the world and agent parameters and Table~\ref{tab:ppo-settings} the training settings. Both objectives use identical settings; they differ only in the drive $D$.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}Xl@{}}
\toprule
Parameter & Value\\
\midrule
Grid / boundary & $50\times50$ tiles / toroidal\\
Tick / speed / maximum turn & 0.05\,s / 5 tiles\,s$^{-1}$ / $10^\circ$ per tick\\
Passive loss & 2 per second\\
Traversal cost $c(n)$ & $1+4e^{-n/3}$ per second; entry counts fade with half-life 200\,s\\
Patches / size / quality & 10 / $3\times3$ cells / uniform $[3,15]$\\
Regrowth delay & $8\,\mathrm{s}/f$\\
Degradation per harvest / fertility recovery & $f\leftarrow0.92f$ / time constant 300\,s (changing world only)\\
New patches (changing world) & 2, quality uniform $[8,12]$, no degradation, regrowth $\times0.5$, appear at 300\,s $\ge$20 tiles from the agent\\
Perception & 7 rays over $90^\circ$, range 10 tiles\\
Belief precision from sighting / contact / harvest & 0.05 per tick / 0.5\,s$^{-1}$ / 2\\
Precision decay time constant & 200\,s (low-precision reference: 20\,s, no harvest precision)\\
Believed regrowth time & 8\,s\\
Phases & 300\,s / 100\,s / 300\,s; $h$ continuous; $h_0=0$\\
Trap criterion & $h\le-100$ during the phase\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\caption{World and agent parameters.}
\label{tab:world-settings}
\end{table}
