\subsection{Implementation settings}\label{app:implementation-settings}

Table~\ref{tab:world-settings} lists the world and agent parameters and Table~\ref{tab:ppo-settings} lists the training settings. HRL and NF use identical settings; they differ only in their reward functions.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering\small
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.15}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.34\linewidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X@{}}
\toprule
Parameter & Value\\
\midrule
Grid / boundary & $50\times50$ tiles / toroidal\\
Tick / speed / maximum turn & 0.05\,s / 5 tiles/s / $10^\circ$ per tick\\
Passive loss & 2 per second\\
Traversal cost $c(n)$ & $1+4e^{-n/3}$ per second; entry counts fade with half-life 200\,s\\
Patches / size / quality & 10 / $3\times3$ cells / uniform $[3,15]$\\
Regrowth delay & $8\,\mathrm{s}/f$\\
Degradation per harvest / fertility recovery & $f\leftarrow0.92f$ / time constant 300\,s (changing world only)\\
New patches (changing world) & 2, quality uniform $[8,12]$, no degradation, regrowth $\times0.5$, appear at 300\,s $\ge$20 tiles from the agent\\
Perception & 7 rays over $90^\circ$, range 10 tiles\\
Belief precision from sighting / contact / harvest & 0.05 per tick / 0.5/s / 2\\
Precision decay time constant & 200\,s (low-precision reference: 20\,s, no harvest precision)\\
Believed regrowth time & 8\,s\\
Phases & 300\,s / 100\,s / 300\,s; $h$ continuous; $h_0=0$\\
Trap criterion & $h\le-100$ during the phase\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\caption{World and agent parameters.}
\label{tab:world-settings}
\end{table}

Network & 10 inputs and a bias, two tanh layers of 64, policy (4) and value heads\\
Reward per tick & $\kappa(D_{t-1}-D_t)$, $\kappa=\Delta t/(1-\gamma)$, $\gamma=0.99$; HRL $D=|h|/25$, NF $D=\max(-h,0)/25$\\
Option discount / GAE $\lambda$ & $0.99^{10}$ / 0.95\\
Optimizer / learning rate & Adam / $3\times10^{-4}$\\
Clip / epochs / minibatch & 0.2 / 4 / 2048\\
Entropy weight / value weight / gradient clip & 0.01 / 0.5 / 0.5\\
Training & 60 iterations $\times$ 16 trials in the stable world; four training seeds per objective\\
Checkpoint selection & every fifth iteration from 20 and the last; 8 held-out stable-world trials; HRL lowest mean $|h|$, NF lowest mean deficit\\
Evaluation & 64 paired trials per training seed (seeds 5000--5063), sampled actions\\
